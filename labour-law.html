Labour Law Services

Expert guidance to keep your business compliant and your employees protected

Employment Contracts

We draft, review, and update employment contracts to ensure full compliance with the Kenya Employment Act 2007 and its amendments.

Disciplinary & Grievance

Structured procedures for handling disciplinary matters and employee grievances in a fair, documented, and legally compliant manner.

OSHA & WIBA Compliance

We help you meet Occupational Safety & Health Act requirements and ensure your workplace injury compensation processes are in order.

HR Policy Development

Comprehensive HR policies and employee handbooks that clearly communicate rules, rights, and responsibilities to your entire team.

Labour Dispute Resolution

Expert representation and mediation support for employment disputes before the Employment and Labour Relations Court (ELRC).

Compliance Training

We train your HR team and line managers on Kenyan labour law, ensuring everyone understands their legal obligations and rights.

Key Kenyan Labour Laws We Cover

Employment Act, 2007
Labour Relations Act, 2007
Work Injury Benefits Act (WIBA)
Occupational Safety & Health Act
National Social Security Fund Act
National Hospital Insurance Fund Act
